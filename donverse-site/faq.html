Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about our apps, your data and how to reach us. If yours is not here, write to us.

Who is Donverse?

Donverse is a small software company based in Hansi, Haryana, India. We build mobile apps and publish them on Google Play. We are registered in India as a micro enterprise under Udyam number UDYAM-HR-06-0094457.

Where can I download your apps?

Our apps are published on Google Play. Search for "Donverse" as the developer name, or follow the link on the app page you were sent. We only publish apps through Google Play, so please be careful with files or links from other sources that claim to be ours.

Are your apps free?

Some features may be free and others may be paid. The price and what you get are always shown before you pay. Every purchase goes through Google Play, so you can see it in your Google Play order history.

Do I need an account to use your apps?

It depends on the app. Apps that save your data or sync it across devices will ask you to create an account. Apps that work fully on your phone may not need one. The app's Google Play page will tell you what it collects.

What information do you collect about me?

Mainly what you give us (such as your name and email if you create an account) and some technical data from your device, like the phone model, app version and crash reports. The full list is in our Privacy Policy, and each app also has a Data safety section on its Google Play page.

Do you sell my personal information?

No. We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with companies that help us run the apps, such as hosting and crash reporting services, or when the law requires it.

Why does an app ask for permissions?

An app asks for a permission, such as camera or notifications, only when a feature needs it, and it tells you why when it asks. You can say no, and you can change your choice any time in your phone's Settings, under Apps and then Permissions. Some features may not work without the permission.

How do I delete my account and data?

Email us at donverse.in@gmail.com with the subject "Account deletion request" and tell us the app name and the email you signed up with. We confirm within three working days and delete your data within 30 days. The steps are on the Delete account page, and you can do this even after uninstalling the app.

Will uninstalling the app delete my data?

No. Removing an app from your phone deletes what is stored on the phone, but not the account data we keep on our servers. To have that removed, send us a deletion request as described above.

Are your apps safe for children?

Our apps are not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you are under 18, please use our apps only with a parent or guardian's permission. If you think a child has given us data, email us and we will delete it.

I was charged by mistake or want a refund. What should I do?

Payments are handled by Google Play, so start with Google's refund process in your Play Store order history. Then write to us at donverse.in@gmail.com with the app name and your order number, and we will help where we can.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Open Google Play, tap your profile picture, go to Payments and subscriptions, then Subscriptions, choose the app and tap Cancel. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription, so cancel first.

I found a bug or something is not working. How do I report it?

Email donverse.in@gmail.com and tell us the app name, what happened, your phone model and your Android version. A screenshot helps. We usually reply within three working days.

How do I report content or someone misusing an app?

Email donverse.in@gmail.com with a description of what you saw, where you saw it, and a screenshot if you can. We will look into it and take action where our terms or the law require.

How can I contact Donverse?

Email is the fastest way: donverse.in@gmail.com. You can also call +91 93503 45616 or write to us at 4150, Friends Colony, Near Narang Filling Station, Hansi, Hisar, Haryana 125033, India. Details are on our Contact page.

Will you change your privacy policy or terms?

Possibly, if our apps or the law change. We post the new version on the same page with an updated date, and we tell you in the app or by email if a change is important. You can always read the current versions: Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.
