Privacy Policy

Last updated October 19, 2026

This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, who sees it, how long we keep it, and what control you have over it.

1. Who we are and what this policy covers

DONVERSE ("Donverse", "we", "us", "our") is a software publisher registered in India (Udyam registration UDYAM-HR-06-0094457). Our address is 4150, Friends Colony, Near Narang Filling Station, Hansi, Hisar, Haryana 125033, India. For the personal information described in this policy, we decide why and how it is used, so we are the "data fiduciary" under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the "controller" under laws such as the EU and UK GDPR.

This policy applies to the mobile apps we publish (together, the "Apps") and to this website. If a particular App collects something not described here, the App will tell you before it does, and that notice applies together with this policy. The "Data safety" section on each App's Google Play page also summarises what that App collects and shares.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information you give us

  • Account details: your name, email address, and password or sign-in method, and your phone number if an App asks for it.
  • Profile and content: anything you add inside an App, such as a profile photo, text, files or settings.
  • Messages to us: emails, support requests, feedback and bug reports, including anything you put in them.
  • Purchase information: if an App has paid features, Google Play handles the payment. We receive confirmation that a purchase was made, the product, the date and an order reference. We never see or store your card or bank details.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Device and app information: device model, operating system version, language, screen settings, App version and installation identifiers.
  • Identifiers: an app instance ID, and the Android advertising ID if an App shows ads or measures advertising.
  • Usage information: which features and screens you use, when, and for how long, plus crash reports and performance data.
  • Network information: your IP address, which can reveal your approximate location such as your city or country. We do not use it to track your exact position.

2.3 Information from your device permissions

An App asks for a permission, such as camera, photos, notifications or location, only when a feature needs it, and it explains why at that moment. You can refuse, and you can withdraw a permission at any time in your phone's settings. Some features may stop working without it. We do not read your contacts, text messages or call history unless an App clearly says it needs them for a feature you chose to use.

2.4 Information from other sources

If you sign in with a third-party account such as Google, we receive the basic details that service shares with us, usually your name, email address and profile picture, according to the permissions you approve.

2.5 Our website

This website does not use advertising cookies or tracking tools. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested) for security and to keep the site running. If you email us, we keep the information in your message.

3. Why we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and provide the Apps and their features.
  • To respond to your questions, requests and complaints.
  • To find and fix bugs, measure performance and improve the Apps.
  • To keep the Apps and our users safe, and to detect and prevent fraud, abuse and security incidents.
  • To send service messages such as account notices, security alerts and changes to our terms or policies.
  • To show ads, if an App is supported by advertising (see section 5).
  • To meet our legal duties and to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

We only use your information for these purposes, or for another purpose you have agreed to. We do not use it to make automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

4. Our legal grounds

We rely mainly on your consent, which you give when you create an account, accept a permission or use a feature, and which you can withdraw at any time. We also process information where it is necessary to provide the App you asked for (contract), where the law requires it, and for our legitimate interests in keeping the Apps secure and working, as long as those interests do not override your rights. Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, we also process data for the "legitimate uses" that Act permits.

5. Advertising and analytics

Some Apps may use services such as Google Firebase (analytics and crash reporting) and Google AdMob (ads). These services may collect device identifiers and usage data and may use them to measure performance and show ads. Please see how Google uses information from partner apps and the Google Privacy Policy.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation on your phone under Settings, then Privacy, then Ads. We do not show personalised ads to children.

6. Who we share your information with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only in these situations:

  • Service providers who work for us under contract, such as cloud hosting, database, authentication, analytics, crash reporting, email delivery and advertising providers. They may use your information only to provide their service to us.
  • Google, which operates Google Play and handles payments and app distribution under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Legal and safety reasons, when we believe in good faith that the law, a court order or a government authority requires it, or that it is needed to protect the rights, property or safety of you, us or others.
  • Business changes, if Donverse is merged, sold or reorganised. We would tell you and the new owner would have to honour this policy.
  • With your consent, or when you ask us to share something.
  • Anonymous or combined data that cannot reasonably identify you, for example usage statistics.

7. How long we keep it

  • Account information and content: for as long as your account is open. After you ask us to delete the account, we remove it within 30 days.
  • Crash reports, diagnostics and server logs: up to 12 months.
  • Support emails: up to 3 years after we close the request, so we can help you if the same issue returns.
  • Payment and tax records: as long as the law requires.
  • Backups: deleted data may remain in secure backups for a short time, up to 90 days, before it is overwritten.

If the law requires us to keep something longer, we keep only what is required and use it only for that purpose.

8. Deleting your account and data

You can ask us to delete your account and the personal data linked to it at any time, even if you have already uninstalled the App. The steps are on our Delete account page. We will confirm your request and complete it within 30 days. You can also ask us to delete only certain data while keeping your account.

9. How we protect your information

We use measures suited to the risk, including encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), restricted access to our systems and data, storing passwords in hashed form rather than plain text, and choosing providers that follow recognised security practices. No online service can be completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach affects your personal data, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as the law requires.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • find out what personal data we hold about you and how we use it, and get a copy;
  • correct, complete or update it;
  • delete it;
  • withdraw your consent at any time (this does not affect what we did before you withdrew it);
  • object to or restrict certain uses, and ask for your data in a portable format (where GDPR or similar laws apply);
  • nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or become unable to (India);
  • complain to a regulator, such as the Data Protection Board of India or your local data protection authority.

To use any right, email donverse.in@gmail.com. We may ask you to confirm your identity first. We reply within 30 days. We do not charge a fee unless a request is clearly excessive. We will not treat you worse for using your rights.

California residents: we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising as those terms are defined under California law, and you may still ask us what we hold or to delete it.

11. Children

Our Apps are not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. In India, a child is anyone under 18. We do not knowingly process the personal data of a child without the verifiable consent of a parent or guardian, and we do not track or monitor the behaviour of children or show them targeted ads. If you believe a child has given us personal information without permission, email us and we will delete it promptly.

12. Transfers outside your country

Our service providers, including Google, may store and process data on servers in other countries. When that happens, we use providers that apply appropriate safeguards, and we comply with the transfer rules that apply to us.

13. Links to other sites

Our Apps or website may link to other sites and services. We do not control them and are not responsible for their privacy practices. Please read their policies.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our Apps or the law change. We will post the new version on this page with a new "last updated" date. If a change is significant, we will also tell you in the App or by email before it takes effect, and ask for your consent again if the law requires it.
