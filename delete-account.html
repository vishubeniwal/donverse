Delete your account and data

You can ask us to delete your account and the personal data linked to it at any time.

How to ask

Email donverse.in@gmail.com with the subject "Account deletion request" and tell us:

  • which app your account is in
  • the email address or username you signed up with

We may reply to check that the account is yours.

What happens next

  • We confirm your request within three working days.
  • We delete your account, profile and the personal data connected to it within 30 days.
  • We may keep a few records the law requires, such as payment records, and anonymous data that cannot be traced back to you.

Want to remove only some data?

Email us the same way and say what you want removed. Your account can stay open.
